You are a senior full-stack engineer and QA automation expert.

Your task is to design, implement, and evaluate a feature that directly compares our product against Retell (https://www.retellai.com) in terms of functionality, performance, and user experience.

## Objectives

1. Recreate the core Retell-like workflow:

   * AI voice agent handling inbound/outbound calls
   * Real-time transcription
   * LLM-driven responses
   * Tool/function calling (APIs, CRM, etc.)
   * Call state management

2. Implement the same workflow using our system.

3. Build a **controlled test harness** to compare both systems.

## Requirements

### Feature Parity Checklist

* Latency (response time per turn)
* Voice naturalness (subjective + measurable proxies)
* Interruption handling (barge-in)
* Conversation coherence over multi-turn dialogue
* Tool/API execution reliability
* Error recovery (timeouts, misrecognition)

### Test Harness

* Simulate at least 20–50 realistic call scenarios:

  * Customer support
  * Appointment booking
  * Lead qualification
* Use identical prompts and flows for both systems
* Log:

  * Response latency (ms)
  * Word error rate (if transcription involved)
  * Task completion success rate
  * Number of turns per successful outcome

### Evaluation Metrics

Produce a structured comparison:

* Quantitative:

  * Avg latency
  * Success rate (%)
  * Avg turns to completion
* Qualitative:

  * Response quality (LLM judgment or rubric scoring)
  * Naturalness of conversation
  * Failure cases

### Output

1. A comparison report (JSON + human-readable summary)
2. A scoring table: Us vs Retell
3. Key weaknesses of our system
4. Specific, actionable improvements prioritized by impact

### Bonus

* Add an automated evaluator using an LLM judge
* Highlight where Retell outperforms us and why
* Suggest architectural improvements (streaming, caching, prompt design, etc.)

Be precise, measurable, and critical. Avoid vague conclusions.
