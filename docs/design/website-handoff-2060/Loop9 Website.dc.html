What Loop9 does

01

Sounds like a person, not a phone tree

Natural, low-latency voice that handles interruptions, accents and tangents. Callers talk the way they'd talk to a receptionist — no "press 1 for billing," no dead air.

02

Knows your business cold

Point it at your website, docs and past calls. Loop9 answers pricing, hours and policy questions accurately, and says "let me get someone" the moment it shouldn't guess.

03

Books, routes and follows up

It checks your calendar, books the slot, warm-transfers to the right rep, and texts the caller a confirmation before they've hung up — then logs the whole thing to your CRM.

04

Every call, transcribed and scored

Full transcripts, sentiment, outcomes and recordings in one dashboard. See what callers actually ask for, and where a human still needs to step in.
