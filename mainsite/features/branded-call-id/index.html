The problem

How Loop9 works

Branded Call ID in Loop9 means controlled caller ID from the business numbers you own and verify. Use those DIDs for outbound campaigns and for transfers that should still look professional.

Outcomes for your team

Higher answer rates. Stronger brand presence. Cleaner handoffs that still read as your company.

  • Controlled caller ID on transfers
  • Align numbers with brand presence
  • Pair with verified phone numbers

Questions

Is this carrier CNAM branded calling?

Does this help transfers?

Yes. Configurable caller ID on transfer keeps the handoff professional.
