The problem

Most phone AI projects stall in engineering queues. Operations knows how calls should sound; builders need a canvas that matches that reality without waiting on custom code.

How Loop9 works

Loop9 Build gives growing businesses a no-code workspace to design greeting, discovery, booking, escalation, and wrap-up. Attach knowledge, tools, and transfer rules in the same place so agents stay on-brand and on-policy from day one. Templates accelerate common SME workflows while still allowing full control.

Outcomes for your team

Go from blank line to a tested agent faster. Reduce dependency on scarce engineering time. Keep conversation design in the hands of the people who own the customer experience.

  • Visual agent and flow builders with templates
  • Attach knowledge bases, calendars, and tools
  • Configure transfer rules and escalation paths
  • Preview behavior before you go live

Questions

Do I need developers to build an agent?

No. Ops and CX teams can configure agents and flows visually. Developers can still extend with APIs and automations when needed.

Can we reuse templates?

Yes. Start from templates for common workflows, then tailor scripts, tools, and transfer rules to your business.
