The problem

Prompt-only agents drift. Policy changes require rewrites. Callers notice when answers are wrong.

How Loop9 works

Loop9 Knowledge Base uses retrieval so agents ground responses in your documents. When confidence is low, transfer rules send callers to humans. Post-call analysis surfaces gaps so you improve the corpus continuously.

Outcomes for your team

Fewer hallucinations on hours, pricing, and policy. Faster updates when business rules change. A tighter loop between docs, calls, and QA.

  • RAG retrieval from your documents
  • Fewer hallucinations on policy questions
  • Faster updates when business rules change
  • Pairs with post-call gap analysis

Questions

What content can we add?

Policies, FAQs, product docs, and operational guides — the material your front desk already relies on.

What if the answer is not in the knowledge base?

Agents should transfer or clarify rather than invent. Pair knowledge with transfer rules for safety.
