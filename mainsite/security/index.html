Security & controls

Controls for teams handling real customer conversations

Loop9 is built for operators who need visibility and safe escalation — without black-box AI on the phone line.

Access control

Use role-based access so the right people can build agents, deploy numbers, monitor live calls, and review quality. Keep production changes intentional.

Conversation visibility

Transcripts and call detail support coaching, dispute resolution, and continuous improvement. Supervisors can see what happened — not guess from summaries alone.

Hybrid human handoff

When judgment or empathy is required, warm transfer rules escalate to your team with context. AI handles volume; people handle exceptions.

Quality oversight

AI quality assurance and post-call analysis help you score and review conversations across volume, so risk patterns surface earlier than random sampling allows.

Practical privacy posture

Treat call recordings and transcripts as sensitive operational data. Limit access with roles, review retention practices with your team, and use transfer/escalation paths for sensitive topics your agents should not handle alone.
